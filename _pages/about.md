My ever-lasting research interest is in self-supervised learning (SSL), i.e., how to learn from vast unlabeled data by creating supervision from the data itself. I developed some theories and methods of SSL for pretraining ([contrastive](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.13457), [non-contrastive](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.02387), [reconstructive](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.08344), and [autoregressive](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.13228)), long-context learning ([LongPPL](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.23771)), reasoning ([self-correction](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.18634), [CoT length](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.07266)), and reinforcement learning ([G1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.18499), [SSL4RL](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.16416), and [IW-OPD](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.22600)). In addition, I enjoy thinking about puzzles in neural architectures, sparsity, and AI safety.
